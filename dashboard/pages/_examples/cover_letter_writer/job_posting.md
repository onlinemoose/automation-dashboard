# Product Lead

## About us

We're a coaching-technology company on a mission to make professional
coaching accessible to everyone — from first-time employees to senior
leaders, across offices, stores, factories, and field sites. Our approach
is hybrid: real one-to-one human coaching for empathy, accountability,
and breakthrough moments, combined with an AI coaching product for
always-on support between sessions. Each person's coaching history forms a
private, encrypted record the AI draws on, so guidance is personal rather
than generic.

## Why this role exists

We have strong product-market fit and a roster of well-known enterprise
clients across manufacturing, automotive, and technology. Two problems now
define our priorities, and this role owns both:

- **Long-term retention of the core product.** Pilots don't reliably
  convert into company-wide rollouts. We need to understand why and fix
  it.
- **Activation and retention of the AI product.** The AI coach is live and
  well rated by the people who use it. The challenge is adoption: getting
  people to their first session, then keeping them coming back.

We're not looking for incremental thinking. If you believe we should
retire products, build something new, or change direction, we want to hear
it.

## The team

Product and Engineering is deliberately small — a handful of engineers, a
data engineer, and a designer — focused and fast-moving. The core product
is close to breakeven. Our investors are funding a dedicated push into AI
that will roughly double the team over the next year, and you'll have real
influence over who we hire and how the team is shaped.

## The role

As Product Lead you own the full product experience across the platform,
with a strategic focus on the AI product and the longer-term vision of a
connected system linking each person's coaching history, blended
human-and-AI journeys, and an analytics dashboard for HR. You'll report to
the CEO and work across product, engineering, design, and commercial
teams. This is a leadership role for someone who can think in multi-year
horizons while shipping every week, and grow a team while delivering
results.

## What you'll do

- Define and own the one-to-three-year product strategy for the evolution
  from AI coach to a broader workforce platform.
- Solve the adoption problem end to end: diagnose why some client groups
  reach near-total activation while others stall, and close the gap.
- Keep the AI product differentiated — concise, question-led coaching
  conversations backed by quality checks, not long generic answers.
- Shape an analytics experience that lets HR explore aggregated trends in
  engagement and retention, never individual transcripts; privacy and
  encryption are non-negotiable.
- Run discovery with users across the full range, from enterprise HR to
  frontline workers, and turn what you learn into clear direction.
- Break strategy into roadmaps and backlogs across mobile, web, and AI
  surfaces, and prototype directly when it helps.

## What we're looking for

- 5+ years in product management in fast-moving, agile environments.
- A track record of diagnosing and fixing activation and retention
  problems — you've found out why users don't come back and fixed it.
- Hands-on experience with AI/ML products: prompts, retrieval,
  personalization, and the UX challenges of conversational AI.
- Experience scaling small teams while keeping culture and velocity
  intact.
- A builder's mindset: comfortable prototyping with tools like Figma and
  AI code assistants.
- Strong English; a second European language is a plus. Based in, or
  willing to relocate to, our main office, with regular travel to client
  sites.

## What we offer

- Competitive salary and equity, so you share in the value you create.
- Unlimited coaching plus full access to the AI product.
- An annual learning budget and a retirement contribution.
- Flexible hours and regular team gatherings.

## Hiring process

A screening call, a short case discussion, a written strategy document, a
strategy interview, a brief hands-on prototype exercise, and a team
culture conversation. We want to see how you think, not just what you
conclude.

We're an equal-opportunity employer and welcome applicants of all
backgrounds.
