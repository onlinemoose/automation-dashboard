# Senior Platform Engineer — Northwind Logistics

**Location:** London (hybrid, 2 days/week in office)
**Team:** Core Platform

Northwind moves freight for retailers across the UK and EU. Our platform
team owns the internal services every product squad builds on: the
deployment pipeline, the service mesh, observability, and the shared data
plane. We are 40 engineers and growing.

## What you'll do

- Own the reliability of the platform's core services. You'll set the
  SLOs, run the on-call rotation for the team, and lead incident reviews.
- Lead the migration of our deployment pipeline from a bespoke Jenkins
  setup to a GitOps model. This is a cross-team programme; you'll bring
  the product squads along, not just ship the tooling.
- Cut the cost of our observability stack. It has grown to a six-figure
  monthly bill and the signal-to-noise ratio is poor.
- Mentor mid-level engineers on the team and raise the bar in design
  review.

## What we're looking for

- Deep experience running production systems at scale — you've owned
  SLOs and carried a pager for services that matter.
- Strong with Kubernetes, Terraform, and at least one cloud (we're on
  AWS). You can read and write Go.
- You've led a technical migration that touched other teams, and you can
  point to how you kept it on track.
- You communicate clearly with non-platform engineers and with
  commercial stakeholders when an incident has customer impact.
- Comfortable mentoring and setting technical direction for others.

## Nice to have

- Experience with service mesh (we run Istio).
- Exposure to freight, logistics, or another operations-heavy domain.
- FinOps or cloud-cost work.
